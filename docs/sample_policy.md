# University Academic Policies

## 1. Attendance Policy

### 1.1 Minimum Attendance Requirement

All students enrolled in undergraduate and postgraduate programs are required to maintain a minimum attendance of 70% in each course per semester.

Attendance is calculated based on the total number of scheduled classes for each course.

### 1.2 Attendance Tracking

Attendance is recorded by the course instructor at the beginning of each scheduled class. Students arriving more than 15 minutes after the scheduled start time are marked absent.

### 1.3 Consequences of Non-Compliance

Students who fail to meet the 70% minimum attendance requirement in any course will be deemed ineligible to appear in the end-semester examination for that course.

Such students must re-register for the course in the following semester.

### 1.4 Attendance Exceptions

The following exceptions may be granted upon application to the Dean of Student Affairs:

- Medical leave (supported by valid medical certificate): Up to 15% relaxation
- Participation in university-approved events: Up to 10% relaxation
- Students with disabilities: Reasonable accommodations as determined by the Disability Services Office

Medical leave exceptions require documentation submitted within 7 days of return to class.

---

## 2. Examination Eligibility

### 2.1 Eligibility Requirements

To be eligible to appear in end-semester examinations, a student must satisfy ALL of the following:

1. Minimum 70% attendance in the respective course
2. Completion of all mandatory assignments and lab work
3. No outstanding disciplinary actions
4. Payment of all semester fees

### 2.2 Eligibility Verification Process

The Examination Cell verifies eligibility 14 days before the examination period begins. Students found ineligible are notified via official university email.

### 2.3 Appeals

Students deemed ineligible may appeal to the Academic Appeals Committee within 7 days of notification. The committee's decision is final.

## 3. Grading Policy

### 3.1 Grading Scale

| Grade | Percentage | Grade Points |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| A+    | 90-100    | 10          |
| A     | 80-89     | 9           |
| B+    | 70-79     | 8           |
| B     | 60-69     | 7           |
| C+    | 50-59     | 6           |
| C     | 45-49     | 5           |
| D     | 40-44     | 4           |
| F     | Below 40  | 0           |

### 3.2 Minimum Passing Grade

A minimum grade of D (40%) is required to pass a course. Students receiving an F grade must repeat the course.

### 3.3 GPA Calculation

The Grade Point Average (GPA) is calculated as:

GPA = Sum(Course Credits x Grade Points) / Total Credits Attempted

A minimum cumulative GPA of 5.0 is required for good academic standing.

### 3.4 Grade Moderation

The Moderation Committee reviews results where more than 40% of students in a course receive an F grade. The committee may recommend re-evaluation.

---

## 4. Academic Probation

### 4.1 Probation Criteria

A student is placed on academic probation when:

1. Their cumulative GPA falls below 5.0 for two consecutive semesters, OR
2. They fail more than 50% of their enrolled courses in any semester, OR
3. They are found in violation of academic integrity policies

### 4.2 Probation Consequences

Students on academic probation:

- Cannot hold positions in student organizations
- Cannot represent the university in extracurricular competitions
- Must attend mandatory academic counseling sessions
- Are limited to a maximum of 5 courses per semester (reduced from normal maximum of 7)
- Are ineligible for merit-based scholarships

### 4.3 Probation Duration and Removal

Academic probation lasts for a minimum of one semester. A student is removed from probation when their cumulative GPA reaches 5.0 or above.

### 4.4 Dismissal

Students who remain on academic probation for three consecutive semesters are subject to academic dismissal. The Dismissal Review Board evaluates each case individually.

Dismissed students may apply for re-admission after a minimum gap of one academic year.

---

## 5. Course Registration

### 5.1 Registration Requirements

Students must complete course registration during the designated registration period. Late registration incurs a penalty fee.

### 5.2 Prerequisites

Students cannot register for a course unless all prerequisite courses have been passed (minimum grade D).

### 5.3 Credit Load

- Regular students: Minimum 4 courses, Maximum 7 courses per semester
- Students on academic probation: Maximum 5 courses per semester
- Final year students with remaining backlog: May register for up to 8 courses with Dean's approval

### 5.4 Course Withdrawal

Students may withdraw from a course within the first 4 weeks of the semester without academic penalty. Withdrawal after this period results in a W grade on the transcript.

---

## 6. Scholarship Policy

### 6.1 Merit Scholarship Eligibility

Students must maintain a cumulative GPA of 8.0 or above to retain merit-based scholarships.

### 6.2 Attendance Requirement for Scholarships

Scholarship holders must maintain a minimum attendance of 85% across all courses (higher than the standard 70% requirement).

### 6.3 Scholarship Revocation

Scholarships are revoked if:

1. GPA falls below 8.0
2. Attendance drops below 85%
3. Student is placed on academic probation
4. Student is found in violation of the code of conduct

Revoked scholarships cannot be reinstated in the same academic year.
